AXIOFLOW HOME — PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

Axioflow Home ("App," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.


1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Data You Create in the App

- Household name and app settings
- Family members, including names, birthdays, photos, and any custom details you add
- Tasks and reminders, including due dates, owners, and repeat settings
- Household binder records, including titles, categories, tags, notes, and expiry dates
- Photos and PDFs you attach to records
- Travel plans, including destinations, dates, travellers, bookings, itineraries, packing lists, checklists, documents, and notes

This data is stored locally on your device. We operate no servers, and we cannot see it. It is not transmitted to us, and it is not sold or shared, except through the specific services listed in Section 3.

Camera & Photo Library

If you attach a document, a receipt, or a family member's photo, the App asks for access to your camera or photo library. Images you choose are stored with your household data on your device and are never uploaded to us. Identity and financial documents may show sensitive details, so treat them as you would the paper originals — they are included in your backups.

The Memories section of a travel plan reads photos already in your library that were taken between the trip's start and end dates, so it can show them alongside the trip. Those photos stay in your library; the App only displays them.

Reading Documents (On-Device)

When you attach a photo or a PDF, the App can read the text on it to fill in details for you. This uses Apple's on-device text recognition. The document and the text read from it stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

AI Features (On-Device)

Brain Dump, AI trip planning, and itinerary parsing run entirely on your device. On iPhone and iPad that support it, the App uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model; on other devices it uses a built-in parser included in the App. There is no cloud AI service, no API key, and no account. What you type is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Apple Reminders

If you choose to import from or mirror to the Apple Reminders app, the App asks for permission to access your reminders. This is optional, happens only when you turn it on, and stays on your device — the App reads and writes your reminders through iOS, and nothing is sent to us.

iCloud Sync & Backups (Pro Users Only)

If you subscribe to Axioflow Home Pro and enable iCloud Sync, your data is synced across your Apple devices using Apple iCloud, and your backups are stored there. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). It goes to your own iCloud account. We do not have access to it.

Automatically Collected Information

- Advertising Data: The free plan displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data for personalized or contextual advertising. iOS asks your permission before tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- Purchase Data: Payment for Axioflow Home Pro is processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. We do not collect or store your payment information. See Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- Crash & Diagnostic Data: We may use Apple's built-in crash reporting. No personally identifiable information is collected through this.


2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

- To provide and maintain the App's features
- To process your subscription via Apple In-App Purchase
- To display advertisements on the free plan
- To sync and back up your data via iCloud, if you enable it
- To read details from documents you attach, on your device
- To import and mirror reminders, if you enable the Apple Reminders integration
- To send you local reminders about what is due, if you enable notifications

Notifications are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server and their content does not leave your phone.


3. DATA SHARING

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except as described below:

- Apple (In-App Purchase) — subscription billing. Payment is handled entirely by Apple.
- Apple (iCloud) — sync and backup across your devices. Your app data, in your own iCloud account.
- Google AdMob — advertising on the free plan. Device identifiers and usage data.

The App makes no other network requests. It contacts no map, analytics, or AI service, and it has no server of ours to contact.

Anything you share yourself — a document you send on, or a backup you move — goes wherever you send it, and is no longer covered by this policy.


4. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

- App data is stored locally on your device in an on-device database.
- Attached photos and PDFs are stored on your device alongside that data.
- iCloud-synced data and backups are encrypted and managed by Apple.
- We do not operate any servers that store your data.
- We do not have access to your household, family, document, or travel data.


5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is designed for a parent or guardian to record household information, which may include details about members of their own family. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.


6. YOUR RIGHTS & CHOICES

- Delete your data: You can delete any task, reminder, record, family member, or travel plan from within the App at any time, and you can reset the App completely from Settings.
- Disable iCloud Sync: You can turn off iCloud Sync in the App's settings.
- Skip permissions: Camera, photo library, and Apple Reminders access are all optional. You can decline any of them and continue to use the App by typing details yourself.
- Ad Tracking: You can limit ad tracking in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on iOS).
- Notifications: You can turn reminders off in the App's settings or in iOS Settings.
- Uninstall: Removing the App deletes all locally stored data. Data already synced to your iCloud account is removed through iCloud.
